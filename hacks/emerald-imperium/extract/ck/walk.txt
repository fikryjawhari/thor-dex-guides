S|Split 1: Roxanne
P|Littleroot Town
-|Go upstairs in the first house you encounter and set your clock
-|Go to the house directly next door, go upstairs and talk to your rival
-|Go South to Professor Birch's Lab and acquire your starter
-|Head North to Route 103
P|Route 103
-|Fight your Rival (northern side of Route 103)
P|Oldale Town
-|Head South to Oldale Town
-|Collect and hatch the "Early Game Egg" from the Pokémon Center
P|Littleroot Town
-|Return to Professor Birch's Lab to get your Pokédex
-|Head North to Oldale Town and then West to Petalburg City
P|Petalburg City
-|Speak with the Fisherman at the northern lake to receive the Old Rod
-|Go to the Gym and speak with Wally to get the catch tutorial
-|Go West to Petalburg Woods
P|Petalburg Woods
-|Halfway through the forest, you'll fight the Team Aqua Grunt
-|Go North to Route 104
P|Route 104
-|Fight Dawn at the northern end of the bridge
-|Continue North to Rustboro City and then East to Route 116
P|Route 116
-|Go all the way East through Route 116 and fight Roark
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab
-|Go all the way East through Route 116 and fight Candice
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab
-|Return to Rustboro City
P|Rustboro City
-|Clear the Rustboro Gym and fight Roxanne
-|Speak with the man in the house on the southwest of town to receive HM Cut
-|Leave eastward again toward Rusturf Tunnel
S|Split 2: Brawly
P|Rusturf Tunnel
-|Fight the Aqua Grunt
-|Return to Rustboro City
P|Rustboro City
-|Your rival will fight you when you exit the city to the South
>|You will now be granted access to fast travel via the PokéNav!
-|You can now optionally fight Gardenia at any time, in Petalburg Woods
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab. If you wait until after you've defeated Wattson, she'll have a strong team!
-|Head south through Petalburg Woods to Route 104
P|Route 104
-|Speak with Mr. Briney to have him sail you to Dewford Town
P|Dewford Town
-|Head North of town to Granite Cave
P|Granite Cave
-|Speak with the first Hiker inside the cave to get HM Flash
-|Go through the cave to the top floor to speak with Steven and deliver the letter
-|You may now optionally return to Rustboro City to get some evolution stones from the Devon Corp CEO
P|Dewford Town
-|Navigate the Gym and defeat Leader Brawly
-|Talk to Mr. Briney and sail to Slateport City
S|Split 3: Wattson
P|Route 109
-|You may optionally fight the trainers in the Hot House gauntlet to get gifted one of many gift Pokémon, depending on how many badges you have earned
>|Hot House tab. You may choose to fight at a lower level of badges than you currently own!
-|2 Badges: Tyrogue, Pansage, Pansear, Panpour
-|3 Badges: Misdreavus, Heracross, Pinsir, Scyther
-|4 Badges: Eevee, Magby, Elekid, Smoochum
-|5 Badges: Togepi, Riolu
-|6 Badges: Type: Null (access to all types)
-|7 Badges: Charcadet, Aerodactyl
P|Slateport City
-|Go to the building south of the museum (where the grunts are blocking the entrance)
-|Speak with Dock
-|Return to the museum (get Flip Turn from the closest grunt) and speak with Captain Stern
-|Get the Good Rod just South of town on the West side of the Trick House
-|Head north from Slateport to Route 110, fighting mandatory trainers and your Rival
-|Continue North to Mauville City
P|Mauville City
-|Get the HM Rock Smash
-|Get the Stat Editor (same house as the Rock Smash HM)
-|Get the Bike from the Bike Shop
-|Acquire the gift Pikachu in the Pokémon Center
-|You can optionally fight Fantina any time now, on Route 117 (though you'll have to fight through some mini-bosses)
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab
-|Fight Wally in front of the Mauville Gym
-|Defeat Wattson in the Mauville Gym
-|Access the Game Corner and buy items!
-|Exit town to the West to go through Route 117 to Verdanturf Town
S|Split 4: Flannery
P|Verdanturf Town
-|Enter Rusturf Tunnel and clear the rocks with Rock Smash to get HM Strength
-|Purchase evolution items if you need them
-|Return to Mauville City
P|Mauville City
-|Exit the city to the North
-|Fight Dawn on Route 111
-|Clear Route 111 and Route 112, culminating with Hiker Trent
P|Fiery Path
-|Continue through Fiery Path northwards to Route 113
-|Defeat the mini-bosses on Route 113 to get access to Fallarbor Town
P|Fallarbor Town
-|Gain access to starter eggs!
-|Exit town West to Route 114 and the snow bridge
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab
-|Continue South on Route 114, navigating the mountainous path and heading west to Meteor Falls, where you'll encounter a story cutscene with Team Magma
-|Return to Mauville City
P|Mauville City
-|Return to the North path on Route 111, and go East when you get on the mountain, to the Cable Car
-|Take the Cable Car up to the top of Mt. Chimney
>|This unlocks a PokéNav travel checkpoint to Mt. Chimney
P|Mt. Chimney
-|There are 3 boss battles here: Magma Double, Tabitha, and Maxie
-|Take the Meteorite off the pedestal after defeating Maxie
-|You can now head to the South entrance of the mountain to go to Lavaridge Town
P|Lavaridge Town
-|Purchase a ton of TMs from the mart worker outside the Poké Mart
-|Get the "Mid Game Egg" from the house West of the Pokémon Center
-|You can optionally fight Maylene just Northeast of town (in Jagged Pass)
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab
-|Defeat Flannery in the Lavaridge Gym
-|Acquire the Mega Ring!
S|Split 5: Norman
>|At this point, before or after Norman, you can go through the Route 111 desert, fight multiple mini-bosses in the sand, and get HM Surf
P|Lavaridge Town
-|Return to Slateport City and exit North to Route 110
P|Route 110
-|Find Wally in front of the Trick House north of Slateport past the Bike Route and defeat him
-|Clear 3 floors of the Trick House
-|Return to Petalburg City
P|Petalburg City
-|Clear Norman's Gym and face Gym Leader Norman
-|Return to Mauville City
-|You may also fight Wake, in the water on Route 118, just East of Mauville, optionally at any time now
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab
S|Split 6: Winona
P|Mauville City
-|Head East to Route 118, then North on Route 119
-|Enter and clear the Weather Institute of Team Aqua
-|Return to Route 119 and defeat your Rival
-|Proceed to Fortree City
P|Fortree City
-|Go East to Route 120, and continue across the wooden bridge
-|Speak with Steven to get the Devon Scope
-|Return to Fortree City and fight Gym Leader Winona
-|You can now access New Mauville and the caves across the map that hold the Regi Pokémon
S|Split 7: Tate & Liza
P|Fortree City
-|Go East from Fortree through Route 120 into Route 121, where you'll fight some mandatory rain battles
-|You can optionally fight Byron on Route 121 at this point
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab
-|You'll have a Team Aqua cutscene here
-|Proceed to Lilycove City
P|Lilycove City
-|Defeat Dawn in the city
-|You may go South from Lilycove to reach Pacifidlog Town
P|Pacifidlog Town
-|Get the "Late Game Egg", which contains a random baby pseudo-legendary Pokémon!
-|Return to Route 121 and go South through Route 122 into Mt. Pyre
P|Mt. Pyre
-|Go to the top of the outside of Mt. Pyre, to activate a cutscene with Maxie on Mt. Pyre Summit
-|Return to Slateport City
P|Slateport City
-|Go to the North side of Slateport to the Harbor, where you'll trigger a Team Aqua cutscene
-|Make sure to go inside and watch Team Aqua escape
-|Return to Lilycove City
P|Lilycove City
-|Go Northeast in Lilycove City to find Team Aqua's Hideout at the northern side of the beach
-|Defeat Team Aqua in their hideout
-|Go East through Route 124 until you arrive at Mossdeep City. You'll fight some sun battles here
P|Mossdeep City
-|Get the Super Rod
-|You may optionally go North of Mossdeep at this time to fight Roark, who is guarding access to Shoal Cave
>|Sinnoh Leaders tab. This location features a static Paradox or Ultra Beast encounter
-|Clear the Mossdeep Gym and challenge Leaders Tate & Liza
S|Split 8: Juan
P|Mossdeep City
-|Go East in Mossdeep City until you find the Space Center, where you'll have a cutscene with Steven and Drake inside
-|Go to the West side of town to Steven's house to get HM Dive
-|Return to Lavaridge Town
P|Lavaridge Town
-|Head to the Jagged Pass and discover Team Magma's Hideout
-|Clear the Team Magma Hideout of enemy members, including a back-to-back and a final confrontation with Maxie
-|Return to Mossdeep City
P|Mossdeep City
-|Go East and North from Mossdeep to Sootopolis City to unlock a travel checkpoint
-|Head South through Route 127 and Route 128
-|Use Dive to go underwater on Route 128, to get to the Seafloor Cavern
P|Seafloor Cavern
-|Defeat Team Aqua in the Seafloor Cavern
-|Return to Sootopolis City
P|Sootopolis City
-|There's a cutscene here that you need to watch
-|Speak with Steven and go to the Cave of Origin
P|Cave of Origin
-|Speak with Wallace, then go north of Route 131 to the Sky Pillar
P|Sky Pillar
-|Reach the top of Sky Pillar
-|Defeat Drake to free Rayquaza
-|Go back to Sootopolis City
P|Sootopolis City
-|There will be a cutscene here
-|Speak with Archie and Maxie to make them leave
-|Speak with Wallace in front of the Sootopolis Gym to get HM Waterfall
-|Clear the Sootopolis City Gym, and challenge Leader Juan
-|Head to Route 128 and proceed East to Ever Grande City

-|Defeat Dawn in Ever Grande City
