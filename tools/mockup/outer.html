Collapse style: pick one

  • Sticky header (the usual pattern in phone lists): an open stop's header stays under the search bar while you scroll through it. When you collapse from the bottom, the header is already at the top: it stays put and the next stop appears under it. Costs one header's height of screen while you're inside a stop. Sections and splits snap.
  • Scroll, then fold: a quick scroll up to the header (it flashes as it arrives), then the content folds up into it. One move after the other, no overlap.
  • Snap: no motion. The block closes, its header is at the top and flashes.

What changed in round 2

  1. Top order: Guide info, Next step (EI), Starter, Expand / Collapse all. The expand bars are smaller.
  2. Guide info: the tracker count and Export / Import / Reset all / Reset checklist come first. Then Game docs: cheat codes and "Items with no walkthrough place" are docs now, alongside Hardlove's docs. The intro comes last. The Guide info bar shows caught and failed on both guides.
  3. EI starter picker: picking a starter sets the Littleroot Town encounter, and changing that encounter updates the picker. In the guide, the rival teams would follow it.
  4. Inside a stop: the note, then Checklist, Encounters, Items, Trainers. Tap a yellow header to open or close it. Sections with a single row are wrapped too. Checklist starts closed; the rest start open.
  5. Collapsed headers show done/total, the caught Pokémon or "failed", the item count with a red MISSABLE, and the trainer count.
  6. Several areas in one stop (Granite Cave in split 2, Meteor Falls in split 4): one Encounters section with a box per area, each with its own picker. A short tag (1F, B1F, Stevens Room) replaces the word "Encounter".
  7. Collapse bars at the bottom only appear on a split, stop or section that is taller than the window without the bar. Section bars are small, right-aligned and yellow, so they read apart from the stop bar under them.
  8. Collapsing from the bottom slides: the header starts where the bar was and glides up to the top. It never travels more than one window, however long the stop was. Expand all and Collapse all stay instant.

Try

  • Split 2 → Granite Cave for several areas in one stop; Split 1 → Route 104 or Petalburg Woods for a long stop with collapse bars at the bottom.
  • Pick a starter at the top, then open Littleroot Town in split 1.
  • Switch between 260 × 316 and 537 × 424: bottom bars come and go with the height.
