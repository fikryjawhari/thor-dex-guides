Two choices to make

  1. Walkthrough trainer header. An open trainer in a walkthrough stop is the third level (stop → Trainers → trainer). A its header pins where the yellow “Trainers” header sits and covers it while you're inside that trainer, so it's still two pinned rows. B it pins under “Trainers”: three rows, about 140 px of the 316. C it doesn't stick in the walkthrough (it still does on the Trainers page). Try Split 1 → Route 104 or Rustboro City, open a trainer and scroll through its team.
  2. Item tables, on the Items page and in the walkthrough's Items sections. A rows straight on the page under the header, no box. B each item as two lines: the name, then where to find it. Uses the full width at 260. C the table and its header become one box. As now is the current separate box, for comparison.

How the pages work now

  • Every page: boxes start closed, with Expand / Collapse all at the top. Inside a box everything starts open, apart from the level box. Places and sections stick under the search bar. Collapse bars appear at the bottom of anything taller than the window. Header taps never scroll. The rail arrows go to the previous or next box. All of it is saved.
  • Locations: box (grey) → place (sticky, on the page) → method (Grass, Surf, each rod; sticky under the place). One area per box: its encounter box sits above the places. Floors and rooms of one place share a box (Granite Cave, Meteor Falls, Mt. Pyre, Safari Zone, Magma Hideout…) and each keeps its own encounter box under its full name. Route 101 is box → Route 101 → methods.
  • Trainers: box → place (where the sheet has them: Hardlove's routes and gyms, EI's Hot House by badges) → trainer. Closed, a trainer shows its name, tags and a team preview; open, it adds the full card, whose name still opens the trainer page. An open trainer's header sticks under its place. Expand / Collapse all inside a box works on its places (or its trainers where there are no places).
  • Level box: “Your highest level” is now a closed row under the trainer's notes. It shows the level the team uses (e.g. “15 · level cap”). Open it to change the level. It stays open while you step the number, and is closed again after a reload. The same goes for the walkthrough.

Cases I flagged

  • Grouping rule: neighbouring areas whose names share their first two words. Routes never group, and the four Slabs stay apart. An area that already has several places of its own never joins a box, so Hardlove's Safari Zone Gate stays apart from Safari Zone with its 10 places.
  • EI Fallarbor Town and Mt. Chimney listed “Fallarbor Town Move Relearner House” 27 times (one entry per starter egg). Same-named places now merge into one “Starter egg” list.
  • Places named after another town (EI egg areas): Verdanturf shows “Fallarbor Town Move Relearner House”, Fortree shows “Pacifidlog Town Pokémon Center 1F”. That's where you get the egg, not where you are. Left as-is for now.
  • EI trainer splits have no place headings and mostly one trainer per place, so there's no place level there. The place shows in each trainer's header instead.
  • Hardlove section names mix a sub-split into the place (“Elder Li Split [Level Cap 12], Cherrygrove City”). Shown as-is.

Mockup limits: Export / Import and the reset buttons use browser dialogs, which the viewer blocks here. Search and the Pokémon pages work but weren't changed.
