Round 2: what changed

  • Picked: trainer rows never stick (Trainers page and walkthrough); item tables have no box.
  • Eggs (EI): Verdanturf, Fallarbor, Mt. Chimney, Lavaridge and Fortree are one place each (“Mt. Chimney”) with a yellow section per egg, each with its own encounter box: Fire, Water and Grass egg (starter egg by shard), Lavaridge egg, Pacifidlog egg. Oldale only has its own egg. An egg you already saved on one of those towns moves into its section.
  • Town buildings fold into the town: Littleroot is one place with Starter choice and Hoenn Dex reward. Rustboro, Mossdeep and Sootopolis gifts read e.g. “Gift (Devon Corp 2F)”.
  • Trainers: each mini-split is its own box with its cap (“Elder Li Split [Cap 12]”, “Rustboro Rival Split [Cap 20]”), then a white sticky place, then the trainers. All three levels collapse, with the walkthrough's bottom bars. Expand / Collapse all inside a box opens or closes its trainer cards. EI's boxes come from the trainers' caps and the cap list; the cap 84 box is “Victory Road”. Hot House stays one box, a place per badge tier with its cap. Hardlove's Mini Bosses box lists places without the X−2 prefix.
  • Level row: “Level cap 15”, or “Level cap 15 · yours 17” once you set your own; set it back to the cap and it reads “Level cap 15” again.
  • Round 2 fixes: no gap under the search bar after a resize; a location picked from search opens and lands its place; Gardenia is in the Wattson split (cap 34); walkthrough trainer rows have no collapse bar; a trainer's collapse bar lands it right under its place heading.
  • Items: each box repeats its name as a white sticky heading, with the dashed bottom bar. EI Shops are split by shop under yellow headings (“All free” dropped). Hardlove's Held Items has Type boosting, Battle, Consumable, Gems and Berries. Heart Scales and Rare Candies are one box, Valuables.
