Edit and layout proposal

Click either Edit: both open together at wide sizes. The team area stays put. Residual amounts sit beside HP; tap an amount to open its details in Edit. Open move details to check Field stays below them. These are illustrative damage/stat values, not a live calculator.

Presets expose ability-specific controls: Download, Sword off, Shield off, first-turn checkbox and the full form name. Multi-hit/category is an early layout sketch, not a settled hit-picker design. Field's two columns are a preview for the later trainer-field decision; automation is not implemented here.
