## Sources (what we have, what's missing)

  Battleground. Per Pokémon: ability, item, 4 moves, nature, EVs, "Level at Cap" (Title Defense: "Minimum Level"),
  and "Fight Gimmicks" per battle (Double Battle, Fog, Negative Energy, ...).
- **hzla** (`.tmp/hzla` is a scratch clone at `ff6c3e3`, not pinned yet): `backups/unbound.js` (insane 417 sets /
  97 trainers, expert, difficult 357 / 94: the boss sheets with IVs; title "Unbound 2.1.1"),
  `calc/mechanics/romhacks/profiles/unbound.js` (Vicious Sandstorm, Grass Dash, Slippery Tail, Bellow / Sound Waves,
  full-HP shields, ...), `js/savereaders/savereader_unbound.js` + `save_constants/unbound_2_1_constants.js` (CFRU
  layout, file signature `0x01121999`, ID tables), generated by `scripts/generate_unbound_2_1_save_constants.py`
  **from Skeli's Unbound-Cloud repo**. No Unbound save fixture.

## Decisions

Settled by the readiness check (change only with the user):
- **Data sources:** RomHackDex for the dex (stats, abilities, learnsets, evolutions, moves); the boss sheets are the
  authority for boss teams, hzla for IVs and a cross-check ("hzla says ..." where they disagree, as BB2R); the wiki
  for locations, rates, items, gifts, walkthrough, missions and ordinary trainers. Unbound is closed source: no
  "code wins" rule; differences between sources show as "Wiki says ..." / "Sheet says ..." and go in notes.md.
- **Boss levels** are the sheets' "Level at Cap" (the game scales them to the player); the card says so once in the
  trainer box (not at the top of the page). Title Defense: "Minimum Level".
- **Rival:** one team per starter through `t.st` (the starter picker), like BB2R's rival.
- **Calc:** profile from hzla's Unbound profile (gen 8 base `cfru`), Megas, Z-moves (`zmove`, Unbound has
  Z-crystals); Dynamax only exists in raids: not in the calc. Fight gimmicks (Fog, Negative Energy, ...) go through
  the field review (`tools/trainer_fields_check.py`) with the user.
- **Save:** a new `template/calc/readers/cfru.js` (Gen 3 sections + CFRU blocks 30 / 31, 58-byte box mons), written
  for both Unbound and Radical Red; `cfg.calc.save = "cfru"`. Randomised saves are refused, like hzla's.
- **CFRU family code:** `tools/cfru.py` (naming / forms, Unbound-Cloud ID tables → `GID`, shared RomHackDex
  handling), reused by Radical Red.

To ask the user before unit 3:
- **D1 Difficulty.** Which mode's teams are the default on every card: Difficult, Expert or Insane (the user's
  playthrough)? The other two: (a) left out, (b) collapsed lines like BB2R's `t.normal` (needs `t.normal`
  generalised to named alternates: a template change), (c) a mode picker (new saved state). Recommended: (a) now,
  (b) later if wanted.
- **D2 Ordinary trainers** (310, species and levels only). Recommended: in Trainers and the walkthrough with
  species / levels / location, marked "moves unknown"; the calc fills their moves from the level-up list at that
  level (marked assumed). Alternative: bosses only.
- **D3 Caps.** Caps are the in-game level cap per badge (once unit 1 has the values), splitting the Trainers page
  and the walkthrough like the other guides. Confirm.
- **D4 Missions** (84). Recommended: walkthrough stops at the point each unlocks (checklist steps `stop.s`), with
  rewards in Items; no new tab.
- **D5 Extras:** raid dens (33) as location sections with their species; DexNav / swarms / Gen 8 activation
  (postgame Mystery Gift) as notes on the areas. Battle Frontier, Battleground and Title Defense in the postgame box.
- **D6 Save:** which emulator / save file on the Thor, and can the user copy one Unbound 2.1.x save to the PC
  (`hacks/unbound/.src/saves/`)? Without it the reader is checked against Unbound-Cloud and hzla only.
- **D7 Version:** 2.1.1.1 (the last release; hzla says 2.1.1). Confirm the user's version.
