Calc retest · design study

Fake damage, sampled EI species and abilities, all base items. Six sprites per row. Tap either boxed damage roll to hit the other card; recoil, Helmet, drain and a ready berry follow automatically. Open a move for 16 rolls and exact damage. Bullet Seed has a hit count.

Four moves per side. The alternate preset swaps the player's four moves; HP row restores the initial set. Hail sets Snow for EI. Fight controls are below both movesets, outside Field. Global and the selected side's groups always stack vertically. Collapse Field stays reachable at the bottom of a long open Field. Native selects stay native in both modes, using the Thor app's option list; only Item, Nature and Other ability use full-window text search. Numeric entry uses a bottom keypad in keyboard mode. Compact HP controls retain real-calc density. Damage and speeds are illustrative; Safeguard and Protect are mockup-only additions.
