Calc retest · design study

Fake damage, sampled EI species and abilities, all base items. Six sprites per row. Tap either boxed damage roll to hit the other card; recoil, Helmet, drain and a ready berry follow automatically. Open a move for 16 rolls and exact damage. Bullet Seed has a hit count.

Status moves are a deliberately overfilled switch gallery, not legal movesets. Player/Foe in Field changes the side being edited. Hazards target the opponent; screens target the user. Keyboard mode uses an in-page picker and keypad; typing never needs Android's keyboard. The compact HP controls intentionally match the real calc's density, not 40px targets.
